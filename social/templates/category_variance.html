The Gamble Categories
BLK and STL are lottery tickets game to game — CV above 80%. They swing wildly based on matchup and foul trouble, not just skill.
🏗️
Draft Anchor Stats
FG% and FT% are the most reliable both week-to-week and year-over-year. Build your floor around shooters, then layer volatile upside.
📅
Stream with Caution
Even PTS and REB change ~16% season to season. Don't assume last year's rank holds — role changes hit counting stats hardest.
